Privacy & Accessibility

PDF Tools is a free, in-browser PDF merger and splitter made by Sky Wolf Studios. The short version: your documents are read by the page you are looking at, and they go nowhere else.

Where your PDFs go

Nowhere. When you add a file, the browser hands its bytes to JavaScript running inside this tab, and every operation — merging, reordering, rotating, deleting pages, splitting — happens there, in memory, on your machine. There is no upload, no server-side processing, no queue, and no copy kept anywhere.

Nothing is written to your browser's storage either. We deliberately do not save your arrangement to local storage, so closing or refreshing the tab discards it and starts over. That is the trade: no persistence, and nothing left behind. The only file that ever touches your disk is the one you press Save to produce, and it lands in your own Downloads folder.

How to check that for yourself

Turn off your Wi-Fi or unplug the network, reload this page, and merge some PDFs. It works, because the whole app is cached on your device by its service worker. An app that can run with the network off is an app that cannot be sending your documents anywhere.

If you would rather look directly: open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and use the app. You will see no requests while you work.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address collected.
  • No daily task limit, no page limit, no file-size cap — the only ceiling is your device's memory, and the app tells you when it hits it.
  • No watermark on anything it produces.
  • No ads, no analytics, no advertising trackers, no cookies, no fingerprinting.
  • No third-party scripts. The PDF engine and the fonts are served from our own site.

Tips

The tip jar links to a checkout page hosted by Stripe on Stripe's own site. We never see or store card details. Tipping changes nothing in the app — every feature is free either way, and the tip link never appears while you are working.

Hosting

The app's static files are served by Google's Firebase Hosting, which — like any web host — keeps standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. That log records that a browser fetched the page. It cannot record anything about your PDFs, because your PDFs are never requested from or sent to the host.

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.1 AA: labelled fields, keyboard-operable controls that keep their focus when the page list redraws, visible focus rings, 44px touch targets that grow with the text-size setting, screen-reader announcements for every reorder, rotation and removal, and checked colour contrast in light and dark mode. The display panel in the header sets text size, spacing, contrast, reading style and motion, and those settings follow you across all of our apps.

If anything gets in your way, email us and we will fix it: stephenfurpahs@gmail.com.

For IT and compliance

Static site, HTTPS only, no user accounts, no data collection, no document upload, no third-party scripts or trackers. In normal use the app makes no external network requests at all. Document contents never leave the client.
