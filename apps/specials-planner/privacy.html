Privacy & Accessibility

Specials Planner is a free lesson planner for specials teachers, made by Sky Wolf Studios. The short version: we don't collect anything. Your plans live in your browser, on your device.

What we store, and where

Everything you type — lessons, notes, your schedule setup — is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you're using. There is no account, no sign-up, and no database on our end. We run no servers of our own: the app is a static page, so nothing you type is ever transmitted to us. The app also caches its own files on your device so it works offline.

No student data

The app is designed for teachers and needs no student information to work. If you choose to type student names into your notes, that text stays on your device like everything else — it is never sent anywhere. We collect no student records of any kind, which means there is nothing for us to sell, share, or breach.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no advertising trackers, no cookies, no fingerprinting. The page makes no requests to third-party services in normal use — even the fonts are served from our own site.

Google Drive (optional, off by default)

If a Drive save/load option appears and you choose to use it, the app asks Google for the narrowest available permission (drive.file): it can only see files it created itself — never the rest of your Drive, and never your email. The connection is directly between your browser and Google; your sign-in token never passes through us. If you never tap the Drive buttons, no Google connection is made.

Tips

The tip jar links to a checkout page hosted by Stripe on Stripe's own site. We never see or store card details. Tipping changes nothing in the app — everything is free either way.

Hosting

The app's static files are served by Google's Firebase Hosting, which — like any web host — keeps standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. We don't receive or use those logs to identify anyone.

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.1 AA: the app is built to work with keyboard-only navigation and screen readers, with checked color contrast, visible focus, labeled controls, and respect for reduced-motion settings. Printouts use plain high-contrast text. If anything gets in your way — a control your screen reader mislabels, a contrast problem, anything — email us and we'll fix it: stephenfurpahs@gmail.com.

For school IT

Static site, HTTPS only, no user accounts, no data collection, no student records, no third-party scripts or trackers in normal use. The only external endpoints the app can ever contact are Google's, and only if a teacher explicitly uses the optional Drive feature. Questions welcome at the address below.

Questions
