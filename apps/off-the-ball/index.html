What the defence actually did
A planning board, not a game. Every run and move has a real name, because a name is what lets a teammate read the play before it happens. Defenders act on a delayed view of the pitch and carry momentum through turns, so scouting one predictable opponent and setting how he plays changes what the board tells you. Your own runners look up too: a player heading into a space that has filled will check back or stop rather than run into traffic, and the ledger says so when he does.
