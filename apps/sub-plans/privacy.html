Privacy & Accessibility

Sub Plans is a free substitute-folder builder for teachers, made by Sky Wolf Studios. The short version: we don't collect anything. Your folder lives in your browser, on your device.

What we store, and where

Everything you type is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you're using. There is no account, no sign-up, and no database on our end — the app is a static page, so nothing you type is ever transmitted to us. The app caches its own files on your device so it works offline.

No student data collection

We collect no student records of any kind. You choose what to write, exactly as you would on a paper sub folder — and whatever you write stays on your device. A good rule of thumb: write what you'd be comfortable leaving on your desk for an adult substitute, because that's precisely what this is.

The share link — read this part

"Copy link" packs your folder's text into the link itself — that's how sharing works with no server and no upload. Nothing is stored anywhere; the link is the folder. It also means anyone who has the link can read the folder, so send it the way you'd hand over the paper version: directly to your sub, the office, or a colleague — not posted anywhere public.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no advertising trackers, no cookies, no fingerprinting. The page makes no requests to third-party services — fonts and code are all served from our own site.

Tips

The tip jar links to a checkout page hosted by Stripe on Stripe's own site. We never see or store card details. Tipping changes nothing in the app — everything is free either way.

Hosting

The app's static files are served by Google's Firebase Hosting, which — like any web host — keeps standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. We don't receive or use those logs to identify anyone.

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.1 AA: labeled fields, keyboard-friendly controls, visible focus, checked color contrast in both light and dark mode, and a plain high-contrast printout. If anything gets in your way, email us and we'll fix it: stephenfurpahs@gmail.com.

For school IT

Static site, HTTPS only, no user accounts, no data collection, no student records, no third-party scripts or trackers. The app makes no external network requests at all in normal use. Questions welcome at the address below.
