# Plan for 2026-08-22, written at the shutdown that ran past midnight

Six audits came in (ChatGPT and Grok on Overload, Cross Off and Grocery List)
and the day went where they pointed and then further. Everything below is live
on skywolfstudio.com as of release 04:03.

## What shipped

**Security.** Three injection holes closed, all in the same class, all
reachable from a backup file the app itself tells people to pass around: a lift
id and history in Overload, a page id in Cross Off, a legend colour in Specials
Planner. The last was reproduced end to end through the real interface, renamed
the page and read sibling apps' data, and re-fired on every load because it was
written back to storage. Two of the three were introduced by earlier fixes the
same day.

**The shared-link rules.** A link-holder could raise the entry counter forever
without adding an item, and past 500 every honest add failed: a stranger with
the link could brick a family's grocery list. Written as six attack tests and
proven against a real emulator, then closed by borrowing the coupling the
claims counter next door already used. Also: the shared checkbox stops reaching
signup sheets and caregiver logs, locking a board freezes the two participant
writes that destroy data, and a deleted list stops leaving readable children.

**The fleet.** Three apps were deleting every sibling's offline cache on the
shared origin, the bug that once black screened lucidwinds.com. Fixed, and
guards now runs each worker in a sandbox against a fake fleet.

**Stephen's own bug.** The comfort panel scrolled itself out of its own box in
all twenty four apps because a hidden radio was positioned against the dialog
rather than its chip. Neither auditor found it; it took measuring the boxes.

**Cross Off**, which has a live user: the midnight page-flip no longer opens on
yesterday's page, a failed save says so, the notebook can be backed up, flip
Undo cannot eat work written after the flip, two tabs converge instead of
overwriting, a page takes its timers with it, and the page only ever turns
forwards.

**Look.** The wedding is white and gold, every app signs its header with a
motif from its own subject, half the fleet stopped sharing one page colour, and
every card now carries a hairline frame drawn inside its edge with the app's
flourish in the corners. Four apps deliberately keep plain cards: a 3am feed
log, a medication chart, a caregiver's diary and a supermarket list.

## Lessons that cost real time

- **Ship nothing to a live app without opening it against a real backend.**
  The deleted-list screen passed every emulator test and broke every household
  on the first real open, because renderBoard draws once synchronously before
  the first snapshot arrives and null means "not loaded yet" as often as it
  means "gone". This is the single most expensive mistake of the day and it
  was mine.
- **Adding a field to a sanitiser is where attention lapses.** Two of the
  three injections were introduced by the fixes for the first one.
- **A verifier that cries wolf is worse than none.** The deploy script piped
  curl into `grep -q`; grep exits on match, curl dies of SIGPIPE, pipefail
  calls it a failure. It reported the fixes as missing precisely because they
  were there, and only on the large files.
- **The emulator does not enforce Firestore's 500-write batch ceiling**, so a
  green emulator test can bless a batch production would refuse.
- **jsdom ignores assignment to `localStorage.setItem`**; break
  `Storage.prototype.setItem` instead.
- **Two auditors beat one because they disagree.** Every disagreement was a
  place one of them had done shallow work, and the code always said which.
