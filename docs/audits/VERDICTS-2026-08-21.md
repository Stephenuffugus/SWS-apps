# Audit verdicts, 2026-08-21

Six reports arrived the same day: ChatGPT and Grok each audited Overload, Cross
Off and Grocery List. Every finding below was checked against the real source by
a separate reader, and every high or critical confirmation was then handed to a
second reader whose job was to REFUTE it. Where the two auditors disagreed, the
code decided. The reports themselves are verbatim beside this file.

Twenty agents, fifty seven findings judged, fifty confirmed or partly confirmed.

## The two the auditors disagreed about, and who was right

**The grocery counter (ChatGPT GL-SEC-01 CRITICAL, Grok said no critical hole
exists and the app was production ready).** ChatGPT was right. Written as six
attack tests and run against a real Firestore emulator, the exploit worked:
anyone holding a share link could raise the entry counter forever without adding
an item, and past five hundred every honest add failed. A stranger with the link
could brick a family's grocery list. Fixed the same day, and the emulator now
proves it both ways.

**Overload's plate math (ChatGPT OL-003, Grok verified it as green).** ChatGPT
was right to the decimal, and the verifier found exactly why Grok saw green: the
existing suite tests the plate helper on weights it can represent.

## The one neither auditor found

**The comfort panel scrolled itself out of its own box.** Stephen reported it
from his phone: changing a setting "makes it glitch out and i have to click okay
and reopen them". ChatGPT marked it DEVICE TEST REQUIRED and refused to guess.
Grok guessed at a missing repaint. Both were wrong, and honourably so, because
the answer was in the box model rather than the code. Every option hides its
radio with position:absolute inside a chip that was not itself positioned, so
all twenty one radios were laid out against the dialog, giving it up to sixteen
hundred pixels of invisible overflow. Tapping a chip focused a radio hundreds of
pixels below it, the browser scrolled the only thing that could scroll, and the
title and close button slid out of view. The blank slab left behind is bare
dialog surface, and tapping that closes the dialog. One `position:relative`
fixed it in all twenty four apps.

## What was refuted

Refuting is as useful as confirming, and these are recorded so nobody
re-implements them:

- **CO-008 (stale uid creates duplicate IDs)**: REFUTED. The load path does not
  reissue colliding ids the way the report describes.
- **Grok F3 (service worker asset list may 404 and break install)**: REFUTED.
  The assets are absent from the QA zip, not from the deployed site.
- **Grok F8 (residual dashes in user-facing strings)**: REFUTED. The strings it
  pointed at do not contain em or en dashes.
- **OL-019b (part of the modal focus claim)**: REFUTED.
- **GL-PREF-01 as diagnosed**: the reflow hypothesis is real but minor, and it
  is not the reported bug. See the root cause above.

## Still open, in the order I would take them

1. **OL-005** rest timer can orphan an interval that beeps forever, and logging
   a set is not blocked while resting. HIGH, Overload, mechanical.
2. **CO-005** two Cross Off tabs are last-write-wins and silently lose an edit.
3. **CO-004** flip Undo can erase work done after the flip.
4. **GL-SEC-02 / GL-SEC-03 cleanup**: rotating a share link does not revoke a
   participant who already knows the board id, and deleting a list leaves its
   children behind. The READ side of both is closed as of today; the revocable
   access grant and the recursive cleanup are a design change across four apps
   and want their own session.
5. **OL-003** plate math prints plates that do not add up to the prescription.
6. **GL-APP-01** Grocery List renders a sibling app's board if given its code.
7. **GL-DATA-01** Clear Checked exceeds the Firestore batch limit at exactly 500.
8. **OL-012 / Grok F2** reset-all uses confirm instead of the house Undo.
9. The remaining MEDIUM and LOW rows above.

## Device and human tests nobody can fake

The comfort panel fix wants twenty seconds on Stephen's actual phone: open any
app, step the text size up through every size, and confirm the title and close
button stay put. The rest of the device matrix (rest-timer audio on iOS, the
midnight rollover on a real Android, two physical phones on one share link) is
listed in the reports and none of it was invented here.
