### Full description
(10 Oct, from Astra's review: the unsupported "what you miss comes back sooner" and "metronome" lines are gone; the chord set and memory test lead. Same text as the Drive doc "FRETWORK Play listing, every field to paste".)
```
Tap the fretboard to find notes, build chord shapes, and test what you remember.

Fretwork is a practice room built by a working guitar and theory teacher. Practice on your screen wherever you are. It does not use a microphone or judge your playing technique.

FIND YOUR WAY AROUND THE NECK
Hunt for notes, name a highlighted fret, and find intervals from a root. Choose your fret range and practice settings.

PRACTICE CHORD SHAPES, THEN TRY THEM FROM MEMORY
Work through related triads and seventh chords, explore inversions, and hear how changing one note changes a chord. Then rebuild the set you practiced from memory. Correct notes stay in place, and hints help when you get stuck. See which shapes you got first try and which needed help.

HEAR YOUR IDEAS
Preview a chord and hear it after the previous chord before adding it to your chart. Build your own shapes, see the possible chord names, and keep voicings for later. Set chart tempo and beats, with your own count for any single chord. A starter bank of 31 shapes is ready for standard guitar tuning.

EXPLORE SCALES AND RHYTHM
See 22 scales by interval, move between positions, and play over a backing chord. Play the neck in seven voices with a looper, and practice 3 over 2 and 4 over 3 in the rhythm room.

FIVE INSTRUMENTS
Guitar, bass, ukulele, banjo and mandolin, with alternate tunings, a custom tuning, and a left handed neck.

Free. No ads, no account, no subscription. Your practice and charts stay on your device. Works offline.

From Sky Wolf Studio.
```

## After the first upload
Copy the App signing key certificate SHA-256 from Play Console → Release → Setup → App signing into
`design/play-fingerprints.json` as `"fretwork": "AA:BB:..."`, run `node design/play.mjs fretwork`
and deploy hosting. That is what removes the URL bar from the installed app.

## Build (this box, toolchain in /tmp/bw)
```
cd design/out/play/fretwork/twa
keytool -genkeypair -keystore DEBUG-NOT-FOR-STORE.keystore -alias debug -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -storepass android -keypass android -dname "CN=Debug, O=Sky Wolf Studio, C=US"
/tmp/bw/node_modules/.bin/bubblewrap update --skipVersionUpgrade
BUBBLEWRAP_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD=android BUBBLEWRAP_KEY_PASSWORD=android /tmp/bw/node_modules/.bin/bubblewrap build --skipPwaValidation
```
